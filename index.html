About Litlore Academy

Multidisciplinary Learning & Development Initiative

Learn Better. Dream Bigger. Achieve More.

Academic & Professional Expertise

With a team comprising professionals and academics with experience in PSC and UPSC preparation, finance, English language and literature, nutrition and dietetics, computer applications, and corporate environments, Litlore Academy aims to connect academic knowledge with practical skills and real-world applications.

The Academy also collaborates with industry experts from IT, finance, healthcare, and corporate sectors to ensure that its training remains aligned with current industry expectations and workplace realities.

Litlore Academy is a multidisciplinary learning and development initiative committed to creating meaningful educational opportunities for learners at different stages of their academic and professional journeys. The Academy brings together expertise in competitive examinations, language and literature, information technology, nutrition, and contemporary life skills through structured, accessible, and learner-centred programmes.

Diverse Learning Spectrum

Comprehensive Programmes & Tech Initiatives

Our programmes encompass PSC and UPSC coaching, Communicative English, English and Malayalam Literature, IT and Corporate Skills Training, and Nutrition and Healthy Lifestyle Education.

Our technology-oriented initiatives also include learning opportunities in C Programming, Amazon Web Services (AWS), computer fundamentals, and other relevant IT skills, helping learners develop technical knowledge and digital competence for academic and professional growth. Through guest lectures, mentorship sessions, and skill workshops led by these industry experts, learners gain exposure to real projects, emerging tools, and professional best practices.
